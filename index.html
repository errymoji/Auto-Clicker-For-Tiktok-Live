Step 2: Install and Run the Extension

  1. Open your web browser and go to the Extensions page (you can type chrome://extensions in the URL bar), then turn on Developer Mode at the top right.
  2. Click on the "Load Unpacked" button.
  3. Select the "Auto Clicker" folder that was just created on your Desktop.
  4. Go to your TikTok Live stream and Refresh the page.
  5. Click anywhere on the stream page, then press Shift + L to start the auto clicker. Press Shift + L again to stop it.
🎉 Setup Complete! Your extension is now ready to use on TikTok Live streams.
